4. What ThriveBreak Covers

At no cost to the Facility, ThriveBreak provides and maintains:

  • Brand new smart vending machine(s) — never refurbished or used.
  • All snacks and beverages — sourced, stocked, and priced fairly.
  • Weekly restocking, cleaning, and sanitization of each machine.
  • Maintenance and 24-hour local NJ support for any operational issue.
  • Electricity credit — $25/month fixed credit for Founding Clients.
  • Insurance and liability coverage for the equipment and products.
  • Sales tax collection and remittance on all transactions.

5. What the Facility Provides

The Facility agrees to provide only the following:

  • A 23" × 27" footprint of floor space per machine, accessible to employees.
  • A standard 120V electrical outlet near each machine location.
  • Reasonable access for installation, weekly service, and emergency repairs.

Grand Opening Week — 50% Off

During the first 7 days following installation at each location, all items are discounted 50%. The discount is funded entirely by ThriveBreak. After the 7-day period, standard pricing applies.

6. Service Guarantee

  • 24-hour response for any machine malfunction. If we miss the 24-hour SLA, the Facility's team receives 2 weeks of free premium snacks.
  • Uptime monitoring through remote diagnostics and cloud inventory tracking.
  • Founding Client electricity credit of $25/month, locked in for the life of this Agreement.

7. Cancellation

Either party may cancel this Agreement with 30 days written notice. There are no penalties, no long-term lock-in, and no cancellation fees. Upon cancellation, ThriveBreak will remove the machine(s) at its own cost.
