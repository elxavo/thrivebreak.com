4. Electricity Credit and Payment Terms

Operator shall provide Facility a monthly settlement statement showing:

  • Total machine revenue for the month
  • Facility's $25 per machine electricity credit
  • Net payment owed (if any) to Facility

Electricity Credit Payment Schedule:

  • The first month's electricity credit ($25 per machine) is waived by Operator to help cover the cost of the Grand Opening Week (50% off promotion) and installation.
  • Electricity credits shall commence on the date that is 60 days after the installation date of each machine and shall continue on the same calendar date each month thereafter (the "Monthly Credit Date").
  • Example: If a machine is installed on October 22, 2026, electricity credits begin on December 22, 2026, and then on the 22nd of every month thereafter.

Settlement statements are due by the 5th of the following month. Payments are due within 15 days of invoice. If a machine is non-operational for more than 7 consecutive days due to Operator fault, Operator shall credit Facility an additional $25 for that machine for that month.

5. Maintenance and Uptime Guarantee

Operator shall respond to machine service requests within 24 business hours. Operator shall perform weekly restocking, cleaning, and sanitization.

If a machine is non-operational for more than 72 hours due to Operator fault, Facility may terminate this Agreement with 7 days notice, provided Operator is given the opportunity to repair within that window.

6. Floor Space and Access

Floor space shall be minimum 23 inches wide by 27 inches deep per machine, in a consistent high-traffic location (break room, cafeteria, or similar). Either party may request relocation of a machine with 14 days written notice, provided replacement space meets these dimensions.

Facility shall provide access during regular business hours, minimum 4 hours per week on a mutually agreed schedule, to perform stocking, maintenance, and service. Changes to the schedule require 48 hours notice.

7. Machine Condition and Neglect

Operator maintains the machines in good working condition through weekly service and maintenance. However, Facility bears responsibility for protecting the machines from neglect or deterioration between service visits.

If a machine is found in a state of significant neglect (e.g., no longer functioning due to lack of basic care, exposure to hazardous conditions, or environmental damage not caused by Operator), Operator may invoke emergency service and charge Facility a service fee of up to $150 per visit for emergency repairs beyond standard weekly maintenance.

Facility shall keep the machine's area clean, dry, and protected from direct water exposure, extreme temperatures, or chemical spills. If a machine sustains damage due to Facility's failure to maintain a safe environment, Operator reserves the right to terminate this Agreement with 14 days notice.

8. Vandalism and Tampering Protection

Facility acknowledges that the machines are equipped with AI-powered smart sensors and payment systems that may be targets for tampering or theft by employees or visitors.

Facility Responsibilities:

  • Monitor the machines and report any signs of tampering, damage, or attempted theft to Operator immediately
  • Educate employees that tampering with machines (attempting to override locks, forcing open compartments, or disabling sensors) is prohibited
  • Respond promptly to Operator requests for investigation if vandalism or tampering is suspected
  • Provide Operator access to relevant security camera footage if available (24-hour notice for requests)

Operator Responsibilities:

  • Install tamper-evident seals and locks on all machines
  • Monitor machine telemetry remotely for signs of tampering (forced door openings, sensor disconnects, etc.)
  • Report suspected tampering to Facility and coordinate investigation within 24 hours

Cost Allocation:

  • If vandalism or tampering is caused by Facility's employee or visitor and confirmed (via machine logs, camera footage, or written admission), Facility shall reimburse Operator for repair costs, replacement parts, and labor (estimated $200–$500 per incident)
  • If the cause cannot be determined, Operator absorbs the first incident cost as a goodwill gesture; subsequent incidents are split 50/50
  • Intentional damage that renders a machine inoperable for more than 72 hours is grounds for Operator to terminate the Agreement with 7 days written notice

9. Additional Machines

Additional machines beyond the initial 4 require written amendment to this Agreement and are subject to Operator availability. Operator reserves the right to adjust terms (machine model, service frequency, or credit amount) for new locations based on facility-specific factors.

11. Term and Cancellation

This Agreement begins on the installation date and continues month to month. Either party may cancel with 30 days written notice.

12. Equipment Removal

Upon cancellation or termination, Operator has the right to remove all equipment within 7 business days. Facility will provide reasonable access during business hours for removal. Equipment always remains the property of Operator.

13. No Hidden Clauses

This Agreement contains the full understanding between both parties. There are no hidden fees, no automatic renewals, and no fine print. Any changes must be made in writing and signed by both parties.

14. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Both parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in Middlesex County, New Jersey for the resolution of any disputes arising from this Agreement.
